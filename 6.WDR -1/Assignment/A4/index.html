List of Motor vehicals in the indian market

  • Maruti Suzuki India Ltd :-

    Primarily passenger cars — hatchbacks, compact SUVs, sedans. Models like Swift, Alto, Baleno, Wagon R, Ertiga etc.

  • Tata Motors Limited :-

    Passenger vehicles (from compact cars to SUVs), commercial vehicles (trucks, buses), electric vehicles, also luxury via Jaguar Land Rover etc.

  • Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M) :-

    SUVs, Utility Vehicles, some commercial vehicles, also tractors, motorcycles (via subsidiaries)

  • Bajaj Auto :-

    Two-wheelers (motorcycles/scooters), three-wheelers (especially auto-rickshaws)

  • Hyundai Motor India Ltd :-

    Passenger cars & SUVs. Models like Creta, Verna etc. Also planning / launching EVs locally.

  • MG Motor India :-

    SUVs / hatchbacks / premium features; style & tech focused.

  • Renault India Pvt Ltd :-

    Small cars, hatchbacks, some SUVs; often competing on price + features.

  • Skoda Auto India :-

    Premium / semi-premium cars; hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs.
